About WonderSpark Universe

WonderSpark Universe was created from a passion for children, education, creativity and family learning.


Inspired by real experiences of helping children learn through activities, questions, books and music, our goal is to create a safe, colourful and exciting place where children can explore, imagine and grow.


WonderSpark Universe is the home of WonderSpark Kids, WonderSpark Hill, children's books, educational songs, animations and many more projects to come.
