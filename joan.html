My story

Kind, Curious and Confident

Joan helps guide the younger children through WonderSpark Hill with patience, imagination and a love of learning.

A little about Joan

Joan is the oldest of the WonderSpark friends. She is thoughtful, responsible and always ready to help. She loves asking meaningful questions, reading colourful books, singing, solving problems and encouraging everyone to believe in themselves.

Although Joan is confident, she also understands that everyone sometimes needs support. She reminds her friends that mistakes are part of learning and that kindness can make every adventure better.
