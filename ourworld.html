Our Story

WonderSpark Universe was born from one simple thing: a mother's love for her children.

My name is Annet Kamuli Nawangi. Before moving to the United Kingdom, I worked as an early learning teacher. I have always believed that children learn best through curiosity, imagination, play, encouragement and love.

The idea began at home with my own children. When they returned from nursery with questions, we used paper, crayons, books and different activities to continue learning together.

I began imagining one trusted place where children could learn, read, create, sing, play and explore. That idea grew into the WonderSpark Kids app, children's books, original music, animated characters and WonderSpark Hill.

The name WonderSpark represents the excitement of childhood, the magic of discovering something new and the spark of curiosity that lives inside every child.

Our mission: To help children learn with joy, grow with confidence and keep the wonder of childhood alive.
