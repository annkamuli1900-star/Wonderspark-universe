A little about Jesse

Jesse is a happy and curious younger brother who enjoys counting, playing games, discovering animals and asking lots of questions. He approaches every new activity with excitement and enjoys learning alongside his family and friends.

Jesse believes that learning should be fun. Even when something feels difficult, he keeps trying and celebrates every little achievement with a bright smile.
