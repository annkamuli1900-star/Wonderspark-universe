Our Story

WonderSpark Universe was born from one simple thing: a mother's love for her children.


My name is Annet Kamuli. Before moving to the United Kingdom, I worked as an early learning teacher. I have always believed that children learn best through curiosity, imagination, play and love.


The name WonderSpark represents the excitement of childhood, the magic of discovering something new and the spark of curiosity that lives inside every child.


The idea began at home with my own children. When they started nursery, they came home with many questions. We used papers, crayons, colouring activities and learning resources, but things were often misplaced. I began imagining one place where children could learn, read, create, sing and play.


My second child's love of reading inspired the books, while my eldest, who is pursuing music, inspired the musical side of WonderSpark. Watching all three of my children grow taught me that every child learns differently and deserves joyful, engaging learning experiences.


Today, WonderSpark Universe is growing into a complete children's world bringing together educational apps, books, songs, animation, creative activities and merchandise.


Our mission is to help children learn with joy, grow with confidence and keep the wonder of childhood alive.


Welcome to WonderSpark Universe — where every child can imagine, learn and shine.
