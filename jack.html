A little about Jack

Jack is an energetic young explorer who enjoys reading books, asking questions and discovering how the world works. He loves stories about magical beanstalks and imagines climbing high above the clouds to discover new lands.

Jack is brave, friendly and always ready to help his friends. He reminds everyone that reading can take you anywhere and that great adventures often begin with one curious question.
